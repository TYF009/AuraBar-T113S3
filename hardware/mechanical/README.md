# Mechanical references

The owner supplied a PCB STEP and two enclosure STL parts for visual inspection. The PCB body is approximately 75×38 mm. Both enclosure parts have model-coordinate extents near 194.47×45.20, with heights around 6.60 and 10.50. STL files have no declared physical unit; their millimetre interpretation and assembly transforms are not a completed fit validation.

Enclosure reference supplied by the owner: [MakerWorld curved-screen enclosure](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1598275-xiaozhi-curved-screen-enclosure#profileId-1684127). The source model's redistribution terms and exact revision are not recorded here, so the original third-party STL files are referenced rather than added under the electronics license.

The supplied second STL has multiple connected shells and is not watertight as a whole; that inspection alone does not establish printability. Original geometry was not repaired or altered.

The polished product images use a redesigned housing and UI. They are [concept visuals](../../assets/README.md), not a guarantee that the existing electronics fit the illustrated envelope. A manufacturing package would need verified screen geometry, hole/interface locations, assembly clearances and source-model provenance.
